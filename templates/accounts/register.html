Terms & Community Guidelines

1. Purpose of Dona.Com

Dona.Com is a community platform dedicated to sharing information about 100% free food distributions, langars, temple mahaprasad, celebrations, and NGO food distribution programs. All listings must offer food completely free of cost.

2. Prohibition of Commercial Transactions

No fees, donations, ticket sales, or commercial charges may be demanded from attendees receiving food at any event listed on Dona.Com.

3. Accurate Information

When submitting a free food event, you agree to provide truthful, accurate details regarding the event date, operating hours, exact venue location, and food items provided.

4. Food Safety & Hygiene

Organizers and contributors must ensure that all food shared meets standard hygiene and safe handling practices. Food must be fresh and fit for consumption.

5. Community Moderation & Review

All submissions are reviewed by platform administrators before becoming publicly visible. Dona.Com reserves the right to reject, modify, or remove any listing that violates these terms or community standards.
