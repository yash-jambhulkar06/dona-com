Terms & Community Guidelines

1. Purpose of Free Food

Free Food is a community platform dedicated to sharing information about 100% free food distributions, langars, temple mahaprasad, celebrations, and NGO food distribution programs. All listings must offer food completely free of cost.

2. Prohibition of Commercial Charges

No fees, donations, ticket sales, or commercial charges may be demanded from attendees receiving food at any event listed on Free Food.

3. Accurate Information

When submitting a free food event, you agree to provide truthful, accurate details regarding the event date, operating hours, exact venue location, and food items provided.
